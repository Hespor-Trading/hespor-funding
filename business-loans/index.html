Overview

A business loan built for speed, not paperwork

Known in Canada as a short-term business loan or working capital loan, this funding can also be used as equipment financing.

A term business loan gives you a lump sum to use for almost any business purpose: inventory, payroll, equipment, renovations, hiring or a new location. Instead of a long bank application, the lending partner reviews your business revenue through a secure bank connection and can decide in as little as one business day.

You repay through automatic daily or weekly payments from your business bank account. Because pricing uses a factor rate, the total you repay is set before you sign and does not grow over the term.

Best for

  • Buying inventory ahead of a busy season
  • Equipment purchases and upgrades
  • Hiring and payroll during growth
  • Renovations, expansion or a second location
  • Businesses a bank declined or kept waiting

Cost

What a business loan costs

You repay the amount funded multiplied by the factor rate. Factor rates typically range from 1.10 to 1.50 depending on your business; your exact rate comes from the lender after review.

A longer term lowers each payment but not the total. This makes the cost easy to compare: what you see in the offer is what you repay.

Open the calculator

Worked example

$50,000 × 1.20 = $60,000

Repaid over 12 months, that is about $1,154 a week or $238 per business day. Over 24 months the total is still $60,000, at about $577 a week.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What can I use a small business loan for?

Almost any business purpose, including inventory, payroll, equipment, renovations, marketing, hiring or opening a new location.

How much can I borrow?

Term financing ranges from $5,000 to $1,000,000. The amount offered depends mainly on your monthly revenue and the lender's review.

Can I get a business loan with bad credit?

Credit is not the only factor. The lending partner looks mainly at your monthly revenue and time in business, so a business with steady sales may still be considered even if a bank declined it. Approval is never guaranteed and every decision is the lender's.

Can I use a business loan for equipment financing?

Yes. Term funding can pay for equipment such as ovens, lifts, point-of-sale systems or vehicles, with a total cost fixed upfront.

What are the repayment terms?

Terms run from 6 to 24 months, repaid through automatic daily or weekly payments from your business bank account.

Can I pay off the loan early?

Because the total is agreed upfront, paying early does not automatically reduce it. Any early payoff option is set by the lender and shown in your offer, so ask before you sign.

Do I need collateral?

Approval is based mainly on your revenue and time in business rather than property or hard assets. Any guarantee or security the lender requires is disclosed in your offer before you accept.

What documents do I need?

Basic details about you and your business, plus a secure connection to your business bank account so the lender can review revenue. No business plan is required.
