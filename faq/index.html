FAQ

Business funding questions, answered

Straight answers about eligibility, cost, repayment and how HESPOR works.

Eligibility

What do I need to qualify?

Your business must be located in Canada, have operated for at least 6 months and earn at least $10,000 per month in revenue. Meeting the minimums does not guarantee approval.

Can a new business get funding?

Businesses need at least 6 months of operating history. If you are newer, you can still send your details and we will tell you honestly whether there is an option.

My bank declined me. Can I still apply?

Yes. Many businesses that use this funding were declined by a bank or could not wait for one, because the lender looks mainly at revenue and time in business rather than only at credit history and collateral.

Do you fund businesses in the United States?

Not yet. Our lending partner currently funds businesses located in Canada. US owners can leave their details and we will contact them if a US option becomes available.

Which provinces do you serve?

Businesses located anywhere in Canada can send a request, including British Columbia, Alberta, Ontario, Quebec and the rest of the country.

Cost and repayment

What does business funding cost?

Term funding and merchant cash advances use a factor rate: the amount funded multiplied by the factor equals your total repayment, for example $50,000 at 1.20 means $60,000. The total is fixed upfront.

What is a factor rate?

A decimal, usually between 1.10 and 1.50, multiplied by the amount funded to give the total repayment. Unlike interest, it does not grow over time.

How is repayment collected?

Automatically from your business bank account, daily or weekly, or as a percentage of card sales, depending on the product.

Can I repay early?

Because the total is agreed upfront, paying early does not automatically lower it. Any early payoff option is set by the lender and shown in your offer.

Is this cheaper than a bank loan?

No. Fast funding costs more than a bank loan. It suits businesses that need speed or were declined; if a bank loan works for you, it is usually the cheaper choice.

Process

How fast can I get funded?

Decisions come in as little as 1 business day and funds as fast as 24 hours after approval. Most businesses finish the whole process within a week.

What documents do I need?

Basic business details and a secure connection to your business bank account. No business plan is required.

Do I need collateral?

Approval is based mainly on revenue and time in business, not on property. Any guarantee or security the lender requires is disclosed in your offer before you accept.

Is there any obligation?

No. Sending a request is free and you are never obliged to accept an offer.

About HESPOR

Is HESPOR the lender?

No. HESPOR Trading Ltd. is an independent introducer. Our lending partner in Vancouver reviews the application, makes the decision and provides the funds.

Does HESPOR charge me a fee?

No. Our referral commission is paid by the lending partner and is never added to your cost.

Can I get service in French?

Yes. We serve business owners in English and French across Canada, and this site is available in both languages.

How is my information used?

Only to respond to your request and, with your consent, to let our lending partner review it. We never sell your information.

Still have a question?

Email info@hespor.com, or send a request and ask us directly. We reply within one business day.
