How to qualify for a small business loan in Canada

By HESPOR Funding · Updated November 2026

The minimum requirementsWhat lenders look atHow to prepareIf you are not there yetCheck in 30 seconds

Short answer: For fast alternative funding through HESPOR's lending partner, your business needs to be located in Canada, operating for at least 6 months and earning at least $10,000 a month. The lender reviews real revenue through a secure bank connection, so preparation is simple.

The minimum requirements

  • Location: the business is located in Canada.
  • Time in business: at least 6 months in operation.
  • Revenue: at least $10,000 per month on average.

Meeting the minimums does not guarantee approval; the lender makes every decision.

What lenders look at

Banks focus heavily on credit history, collateral and years of financial statements. Alternative lenders focus mainly on current revenue, how consistent it is and how long the business has operated. That is why decisions can come in as little as one business day.

How to prepare

  1. Know roughly how much you need and what it is for.
  2. Have online banking access for your business account so you can connect it securely.
  3. Keep government-issued ID handy in case the lender asks.
  4. Check your recent revenue so your request is accurate.

If you are not there yet

If your business is under 6 months old or below $10,000 a month, government-backed programs or your bank may be better routes for now. You can still send your details and we will tell you honestly whether there is a fit.
