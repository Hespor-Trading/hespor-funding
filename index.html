Plain language

Key terms, explained

Merchant cash advance
A lump sum a business receives in exchange for a share of its future sales. It is repaid automatically, as a percentage of card sales or through fixed daily or weekly payments, until a total agreed upfront is paid.
Factor rate
A decimal, usually between 1.10 and 1.50, multiplied by the amount funded to give the total repayment. $50,000 at 1.20 means $60,000 repaid in total. Unlike interest, the total does not grow over time.
Working capital
The cash a business has for day-to-day operations such as inventory, payroll and rent. Working capital funding adds cash for these needs during slow months, busy seasons or while waiting for customers to pay.
FAQ

Questions business owners ask

What do I need to qualify for business funding?

Your business must be located in Canada, have operated for at least 6 months and earn at least $10,000 per month in revenue. Meeting the minimums does not guarantee approval; every decision is made by the lender.

How fast can I get funded?

We introduce you to our lending partner the same business day. The lender makes decisions in as little as 1 business day and can send funds as fast as 24 hours after approval; most businesses finish the whole process within a week.

How much can my business get?

Term financing ranges from $5,000 to $1,000,000 and a revolving line of credit from $7,500 to $125,000. Your offer depends on your revenue and the lender's review.

Is HESPOR a lender?

No. HESPOR Trading Ltd. is an independent introducer. We connect your business with our lending partner in Vancouver, which reviews the application, makes the decision and provides the funds.

Does HESPOR charge a fee?

No. Our referral commission is paid by the lending partner and is never added to your cost. Applying is free and there is no obligation to accept an offer.

What does it cost?

Term funding and merchant cash advances use a factor rate: you repay the amount funded multiplied by the factor, for example $50,000 at 1.20 means $60,000 in total. The total is fixed upfront and does not grow with the term.
