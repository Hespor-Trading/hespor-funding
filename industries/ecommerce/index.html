Overview

Why online sellers use fast funding

Online sellers must buy inventory and pay for advertising before the sales arrive. Running out of stock in peak season costs more than the funding ever would.

Working capital lets you order earlier, order more and scale advertising when it is working. The lending partner reviews your real revenue through a secure bank connection.

Example

$50,000 × 1.20 = $60,000

You receive $50,000 and repay $60,000 in total, a fixed amount agreed upfront. Illustration only; your offer comes from the lender.

FAQ

E-commerce financing FAQ

Can Amazon and Shopify sellers get funding?

Yes. Canadian e-commerce businesses meeting the minimums can request term financing or other options.

Can I use funding for advertising?

Yes. Working capital can be used for inventory, marketing and other business expenses.

Does an online business need a physical location?

The business must be located in Canada; the lending partner confirms any other requirements during review.
