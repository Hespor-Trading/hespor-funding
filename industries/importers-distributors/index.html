Overview

Why importers use fast funding

Importers pay suppliers, freight and duties long before customers pay them. That gap can tie up cash for months, especially when orders grow. Working capital funding bridges the time between paying for goods and getting paid for them.

Because import purchases are planned and lumpy, a term loan with a fixed total cost is often the clearest fit. A line of credit can add a buffer for recurring orders.

Example

$150,000 × 1.20 = $180,000

You receive $150,000 and repay $180,000 in total, a fixed amount agreed upfront. Illustration only; your offer comes from the lender.

FAQ

Importers & distributors financing FAQ

Can importers in Canada get working capital funding?

Yes. Canadian importers and distributors meeting the minimums can request term financing or a line of credit.

Can I use funding to pay customs duties and freight?

Yes. Working capital can be used for duties, freight, supplier deposits and other landing costs.

How much can an importer get?

Term financing ranges from $5,000 to $1,000,000 depending on revenue and the lender's review.
