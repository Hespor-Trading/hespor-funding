Overview

Why restaurant owners use fast funding

Restaurants run on thin margins and fast-moving cash. A walk-in cooler fails, a patio season approaches, or a supplier offers a bulk discount, and the bank wants weeks. Restaurant owners use fast funding to act on the day it matters.

Because many restaurants take most payments by card, a merchant cash advance is a natural fit: repayment can follow your card sales, so quieter weeks mean smaller payments.

Example

$40,000 × 1.22 = $48,800

You receive $40,000 and repay $48,800 in total, a fixed amount agreed upfront. Illustration only; your offer comes from the lender.

FAQ

Restaurants & cafés financing FAQ

Can a restaurant get a merchant cash advance?

Yes. Restaurants with steady card sales are a common fit for a merchant cash advance, which can be repaid as a share of card revenue.

Can I finance restaurant equipment?

Yes. A term business loan can fund kitchen equipment, renovations and other purchases, with a fixed total cost agreed upfront.

Do I qualify if my restaurant is new?

The lending partner requires at least 6 months in operation and at least $10,000 in monthly revenue.
