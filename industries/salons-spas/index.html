Overview

Why salon and spa owners use fast funding

Beauty businesses grow by investing in their space, their equipment and their team. A renovation or a new treatment device can lift revenue, but only if you can fund it now.

Most salons and spas are paid by card, which makes a merchant cash advance a practical option: repayment can follow your daily sales.

Example

$30,000 × 1.25 = $37,500

You receive $30,000 and repay $37,500 in total, a fixed amount agreed upfront. Illustration only; your offer comes from the lender.

FAQ

Salons & spas financing FAQ

Can a salon get a merchant cash advance?

Yes. Salons and spas with steady card sales are a common fit for a merchant cash advance.

Can I finance spa or aesthetic equipment?

Yes. A term loan can fund equipment purchases with a fixed total cost agreed upfront.

Do barbershops qualify?

Yes, if they meet the minimums: located in Canada, 6+ months in operation and $10,000+ monthly revenue.
