Overview

Flexible access to capital when you need it

A business line of credit gives you an approved limit you can draw from at any time. You only use what you need, and as you repay, that amount becomes available again. It is the easiest way to keep a cushion for slow months, supplier deposits or unexpected repairs.

The lending partner reviews your revenue and time in business to set your limit. Pricing and repayment terms for the line of credit are set by the lender and shown clearly in your offer before you accept.

Best for

  • Seasonal businesses with slow months
  • Covering supplier deposits and timing gaps
  • Unexpected repairs or opportunities
  • Owners who want funds ready without using them yet

Line of credit vs. loan

When to choose a line of credit

Choose a line of credit when your need is ongoing or unpredictable. Choose a term loan or merchant cash advance when you need a larger lump sum for a specific purchase.

Line of credit

Ongoing access up to $125,000. Draw, repay and draw again. Best for recurring gaps and safety.

Business loan

A lump sum up to $1,000,000 with a fixed total cost. Best for a defined investment.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is a business line of credit?

A revolving credit limit your business can draw from when needed. As you repay what you used, the funds become available again.

How large can the line of credit be?

Lines of credit range from $7,500 to $125,000, set by the lending partner based mainly on your revenue and time in business.

What does a line of credit cost?

Pricing and repayment terms for the line of credit are set by the lender and disclosed in your offer before you accept.

Line of credit or term loan?

A line of credit suits ongoing or unpredictable needs; a term loan suits a single larger purchase with a fixed total cost.

Who qualifies?

Businesses located in Canada with at least 6 months in operation and at least $10,000 per month in revenue. Approval is the lender's decision.
